Should you bring on co-founders?

Honest take: you can build the MVP solo. Co-founders dilute equity — only bring someone in if they fill a gap you genuinely can't cover and they're committed full-time.

Marketing / growth

You can build the product. Getting runners to find it and trust it is a different skill set. Someone with a fitness content audience or community ties is high-value.

Worth considering early
Coaching expertise

A certified running coach as an advisor (not necessarily co-founder) adds credibility to grant applications and helps encode real coaching logic into your prompts.

Advisor first, not co-founder
